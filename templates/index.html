System Methodology

5-Stage CNN Processing Pipeline

Designed according to the MCA mini project synopsis workflow to automate leaf image analysis from raw capture to agronomic prescription.

📸
STAGE 01

Leaf Capture

User uploads or drag-and-drops leaf photograph or chooses sample preset.

⚙️
STAGE 02

Preprocessing

224x224 RGB normalization, color histogram analysis and noise elimination.

🧠
STAGE 03

CNN Classifier

Deep Conv2D feature learning identifies leaf textures, margins, and spot rings.

🔥
STAGE 04

Grad-CAM Heatmap

Attention saliency overlays highlight the precise infected lesions on the leaf.

📋
STAGE 05

Agronomy Rx

Provides verified organic & chemical treatments, audio guide, and printable card.
